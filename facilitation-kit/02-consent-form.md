## 1. What this study is about

We are studying how developers think about working with **multiple AI
assistants on one project**. You will use a small note-taking app and complete a
few realistic coding tasks while thinking aloud. There are **no right or wrong
answers** — we are studying *your* expectations and reasoning, not your skill.

The session lasts about **90 minutes**.

## 2. What you'll be asked to do

- Complete several short tasks using AI coding tools.
- Think aloud as you work.
- Answer questions about what you expected to happen.
- Sketch a simple diagram of how you think the system is organised.

- Findings are reported in **aggregate** or with de-identified quotes.
- We will not share raw recordings outside the immediate research/product team.
- You may ask us to delete your data at any time up to the point of analysis.
